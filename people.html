Director & Principal Consultant

Ken Stapleton

AssDip Mining Eng

Ken is a mining engineer with over 35 years of experience in open-pit mine operations, planning, cost estimation, mining contract management and project development. He has worked all over the world — across Australia, Papua New Guinea, Indonesia and New Caledonia — with experience spanning coal, nickel, gold and copper mining.

Director

David Krinke

BE(Mining) · Grad DipBus · Grad DipEngSc (Petroleum) · MAusIMM (CP) · RPEQ — Mining

David is a seasoned mining engineer with over 30 years of experience in open-cut coal and iron ore mining. He holds a Bachelor of Mining Engineering and graduate diplomas in Business and Petroleum Engineering. As a Director at MineOp, he leads mine planning, scheduling, financial analysis and project management while mentoring engineers. David has provided strategic planning for global mining firms and served as an expert witness in legal proceedings.

Principal Mining Engineer

Vanessa Cox

BE(Mining) · Masters Eng Mgmt · MAusIMM(CP) · RPEQ — Mining

Vanessa is a Principal Mining Engineer with over 30 years of experience in open-cut coal mining. She holds a Bachelor of Engineering (Mining) and a Master of Engineering Management. Vanessa specialises in mine planning, scheduling, financial analysis and operational strategy, with a strong record of delivering strategic insight and engineering solutions across diverse mining operations.

Principal Mining Engineer

Joe Kelly

BE(Mining)

Joe is a Principal Mining Engineer with over 27 years of experience in open-cut coal mining. He has led major projects, including the development of a 13Mtpa greenfield site and the world's first integration of autonomous haulage systems with a manned fleet. Joe's expertise spans strategic mine planning, scheduling and operational optimisation.
