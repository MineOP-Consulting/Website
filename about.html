A leading specialist consulting company, built on a team of highly skilled and experienced mining engineers.

Brisbane's specialist mine consulting services.

Since being incorporated on 27 September 2007, MineOp has worked with a wide range of companies across the industry — from the owner-operator's chair to the contractor's — to deliver accurate, thorough results.

We specialise in feasibility studies, mine optimisation planning and project management. Our team of professional engineers and consultancy experts deliver the very best technical and analytical services in Brisbane, and we bring the same standard to every project we take on.
