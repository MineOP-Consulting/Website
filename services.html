Leading International Mine planning &

MineOp offers technical, analytical and consultancy expertise to a wide range of mining projects across Australia and beyond. Based in Brisbane, our experienced mining engineers and technicians specialise in mine planning and project management — and you can trust us to deliver reliable services and outstanding results.

Mine engineering

Plans & schedules that hold up at the face

Our team of professional engineers provides timely, effective mine plans and schedules. Our Principal Mining Engineers bring extensive experience in innovative mine design, planning and scheduling — and our skill in converting mine plans into contract tender pricing helps us deliver the most cost-effective mining solutions.

Studies & delivery

From concept study to project delivery

We take projects from first-pass concept and feasibility studies through to hands-on project management — keeping scope, schedule and budget aligned so the plan on paper is the plan that gets delivered at the face.
